# @canmi/web

What a SvelteKit app of the author's needs beside its own code: the browser floor and the core-js
fallback behind it (`@canmi/web/compat`), the arrival parameters taken out of the address bar
(`@canmi/web/referer`), and the Sentry wiring (`@canmi/web/sentry/*`). `@sveltejs/kit` and
`@sentry/sveltekit` are optional peers, needed by the Sentry part alone.

Versioned by the day it was released, `YYYY.MDD.N`; see the repository's `spec/repository.md`.
