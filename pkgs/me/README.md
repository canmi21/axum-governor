# @canmi/me

The author's own facts, for the author's own programs: their sites' addresses and the world's
(`@canmi/me/urls`), who they are (`@canmi/me/identity`), the languages they write in (`@canmi/me/locales`), and what every
public host of theirs says to a crawler and an agent (`@canmi/me/robots`). The `canmi` crate is the same addresses and identity for Rust.

Versioned by the day it was released, `YYYY.MDD.N`; see the repository's `spec/repository.md`.
